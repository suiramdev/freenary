Freenary renders **light** or **dark**, and by default it follows whichever your device is set to. Like the language, the choice belongs to your browser rather than to your account: it applies before you have signed in.

## Choosing an appearance

The appearance control appears in the three places where you might need it:

- On the **sign-in screen**, above the form — alongside the language control.
- In the **onboarding header**, next to Sign out.
- In the **sidebar**, under your name, as **Theme** in the account menu.

Each one offers the same three choices:

| Choice     | What it does                                  |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **System** | Follows your operating system. The default.   |
| **Dark**   | Always dark, whatever your system is set to.  |
| **Light**  | Always light, whatever your system is set to. |

Picking one takes effect immediately — no reload, unlike the language.

## What System follows

**System** reads the appearance your operating system reports, so a Mac or phone set to switch at sunset switches Freenary with it, including while the page stays open. Choosing **Dark** or **Light** outright stops that: the interface then stays where you put it until you set it back to System.

<Callout type="info">
  The choice is stored in your browser, so it is per browser rather than per
  account. Signing in on another device starts from that device's system
  appearance until you choose again there.
</Callout>
