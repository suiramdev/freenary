title: Portfolio
description: The financial balance sheet — what you own, where it is held, and what you owe.
---

Portfolio represents the user's financial balance sheet: **"What do I own, where is it held, and what do I owe?"** Every financial account and manually managed asset eventually appears here.

## Hierarchical model

Wealth is organized around four levels:

1. **Connection** — the external institution or provider through which information is obtained.
2. **Account** — e.g. a bank account, brokerage account, life-insurance contract or crypto account.
3. **Holdings** — the individual investments inside an account.
4. **Transactions** — purchases, sales, dividends and transfers explaining how balances and holdings changed over time.

For example, a banking connection could expose a PEA account containing several ETFs and shares as holdings; each purchase, sale, dividend and transfer appears as a transaction. Navigation flows naturally from institution down to individual operations.

## Manual assets

Portfolio is not restricted to synchronized assets. Users can add almost anything that contributes meaningfully to personal wealth: property, private companies, startup investments, precious metals, watches, collectibles, vehicles, employee equity or loans made to others. An open architecture should let the community introduce additional asset types.

## Liabilities

Liabilities receive the same attention as assets. Mortgages, personal loans, student loans and credit-card balances appear alongside the assets they finance: a €500,000 property with €350,000 remaining on the mortgage contributes ~€150,000 of equity, not €500,000 of wealth.

## Allocation

The portfolio overview provides allocation analysis by account, institution, asset type, asset class, currency, geography and liquidity level — revealing hidden concentration behind apparently diverse holdings.

## Account and asset detail views

Each account or asset gets a dedicated detail view adapted to its nature:

- **Overview** — current value, history, contributions, withdrawals, cost basis and investment performance.
- **Holdings** — units, average acquisition price, current price, cost basis, present value, unrealized P/L and weight per security.
- **Transactions** — a common transaction layer shared with Budget, so the same operation never appears twice in different forms.
- **Analysis** — contextual per asset: fees and diversification for a brokerage account; loan-to-value and rental yield for a property; token allocation and volatility for a crypto wallet.
- **Settings** — synchronization status, ownership, valuation methods and connection errors; manual assets can have fixed, updated or market-sourced valuations.
