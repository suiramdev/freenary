Goals transform Freenary from a historical tracking tool into a forward-looking financial-planning platform: not only explaining what happened, but whether current behavior can realistically produce the future you want.

## Goals

A goal represents any user-defined financial objective: emergency reserve, property purchase, retirement, financial independence, children's education, a vehicle, travel or starting a business. Each goal carries a target amount, target date, current savings, linked assets and expected future contributions.

Progress is continuously computed against contributions, expected returns, inflation and time remaining — not as a naive linear projection. Instead of "you have €30,000 of your €100,000 target", Freenary determines whether you are on schedule.

## Simulations

Sophisticated goals use simulations rather than simplistic projections. Retirement and financial-independence planning incorporate investment returns, inflation, contribution rates and withdrawal assumptions; Monte Carlo simulations estimate the probability of success under different market conditions. AI then translates simulation output into plain language: roughly what the current plan achieves, and how much increasing monthly investments or delaying the target would improve it.

## Emergency fund

The emergency fund is a specialized goal expressed relative to actual monthly expenses rather than a fixed amount: €12,000 of available savings with €2,000 of average essential expenses means about six months of coverage.

Because Budget data is already available, Freenary can estimate essential monthly expenditure automatically and update the safety-reserve requirement when your lifestyle changes. The AI assistant can explain how much cash is needed for short-term security and how much could be allocated to longer-term objectives — while financial decisions always remain under the user's control.
