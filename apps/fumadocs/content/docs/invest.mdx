An Invest or Actions section could eventually provide pathways from analysis to execution. Unlike closed platforms, Freenary does not need to become the custodian of users' assets.

## Integration over custody

Because Freenary is open source, the project focuses on integrations. Investment providers, brokers and cryptocurrency exchanges could expose compatible APIs through which Freenary initiates or prepares operations where legally and technically permitted.

This separates Freenary's intelligence and financial-management layer from the institutions that actually hold funds: users keep their preferred broker while using Freenary as the central interface for planning and analysis.

## Read-first, execute-later

The project maintains a strong distinction between read-only data aggregation and transaction execution. Open Banking connections primarily serve aggregation; any future payment or investment execution feature would carry its own explicit permissions, authentication and security boundaries.
