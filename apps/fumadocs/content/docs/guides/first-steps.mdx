Your first sign-in opens a short setup. You cannot reach the rest of the app before you finish it. This page walks the setup, then names what each screen holds while you have no bank connection.

**Before you start:** you need an account. See [Signing in](/docs/guides/signing-in).

## The setup steps

The setup asks for your country, then offers a bank connection. A progress bar sits at the top, labelled `Onboarding progress`, with the step names `Country` and `Bank connection`.

The header carries three controls, in this order:

| Control    | What it reads                                      |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Appearance | `Theme`                                            |
| Language   | The name of your current language, as in `English` |
| Sign out   | `Sign out`                                         |

<Callout type="info">

The bank step exists only when your instance links banks. Without it the setup holds one step, and `Continue` on the country step finishes the setup.

</Callout>

<Steps>

<Step>

### Pick your country

The step reads `Where are you based?`, over the line `Select your country to personalize your experience.`

The list holds **196** countries. The search box above it reads `Search countries…`, and it matches the name of a country and its two-letter code. An empty result reads `No countries match your search`.

`France` sits at the top, because Freenary holds bank coverage and category rules for France. Every other country carries the badge `Limited support`. You can pick any of them, and every screen stays open to you. Freenary then files fewer of your transactions under a category by itself.

Under the list, Freenary asks `Want to add support for your country?` and offers the link `Contribute here`. It opens the project repository in a new tab.

`Continue` stays off until you pick a country.

</Step>

<Step>

### Connect a bank, or skip it

The step reads `Connect your bank`, over the line `Connect your bank accounts to import transactions and balances. You can always do this later from Settings.`

A searchable bank list sits under it, with the search box `Search banks…` and a `Connect` button on each row. See [Bank connections](/docs/guides/bank-connections) for the whole flow.

Three buttons sit at the foot:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Back` | Returns you to the country step |
| `Skip for now` | Finishes the setup with no bank |
| `Finish` | Finishes the setup. With one connection or more it counts them, as in `Finish (1)` |

`Skip for now` and `Finish` do the same thing. The bank step is the one step you can skip.

</Step>

<Step>

### Land on Home

Freenary saves your country, shows the message `You’re all set!`, and opens Home. A failure reads `Something went wrong. Please try again.` instead.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">

Your country survives the trip to your bank. Come back from a bank page, and the setup re-opens on the bank step.

</Callout>

## Home with no bank connection

Home is the assistant. With a fresh account it holds four things:

- The large Freenary mark, with the accessible name `Freenary's assistant`.
- The heading `Welcome back, {name}`, with your own name in place of the token.
- The line `Ask anything about your accounts, spending or budget.`
- Three suggestion chips: `How much did I spend on eating out this year?`, `Where did my money go last month?` and `Which subscriptions am I paying for?`

The chips stay off until a model is ready to answer. The text box stays off with them. See [The assistant](/docs/guides/assistant).

Budget holds one card while you have no bank account: `Connect a bank account`, with the button `Connect a bank`. No period controls, no figures, no charts and no list render behind it. See [Budget](/docs/guides/budget).

## The First steps checklist

The sidebar footer carries a panel titled `First steps` with a counter, as in `1/3`. Each row links into a section of Settings.

| Row | Freenary ticks it when | Where the link goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Connect your bank` | You hold one bank connection or more | Settings, `Bank accounts` |
| `Set up your budget` | Your budgeting profile holds one line or more | Settings, `Budgeting profile` |
| `Protect your account` | You turn two-factor authentication on, **or** you add one passkey | Settings, `Security` |

The counter is the visible report of your progress. Each row also carries a state for a screen reader, `Done` or `To do`.

The panel appears only while work remains. An account that arrives with all three rows already ticked never sees it. Tick the last row on screen, and the panel holds for a moment, then goes away. It comes back when a row loses its tick, as when you disconnect your last bank.

## What each row turns on

- A bank connection fills Budget with your own transactions.
- A budgeting profile turns on the `Budget vs. actual` chart.
- A second factor or a passkey protects the account.
