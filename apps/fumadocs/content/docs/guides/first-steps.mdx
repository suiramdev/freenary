Freenary asks you two questions the first time you sign in, then puts a short checklist in the sidebar. This page walks that path from end to end.

**Before you start:** you need an account. See [Signing in](/docs/guides/signing-in).

## The onboarding wizard

Freenary sends every new account to a wizard at `/onboarding`. You cannot reach the rest of the app before you finish it. The header of the wizard carries a theme control, a language control and a **Sign out** button.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Choose your country

The first step reads **Where are you based?**. It lists **196** countries with a search box above them.

Only **France** works today. Freenary hoists France to the top of the list. Every other country renders as a disabled button with the badge **Not supported yet**, and you cannot select it.

Under the list, Freenary asks: Want to add support for your country? The link goes to the project repository.

Press **Continue** once you select France. The button stays disabled before that.

</Step>

<Step>

### Connect a bank

The second step reads **Connect your bank**. It appears **only** when the operator configured a bank provider. With no bank provider, the wizard has one step, and step 1 finishes it.

The step shows a searchable list of banks. Press **Connect** on one, answer your bank, and come back. See [Bank connections](/docs/guides/bank-connections) for the whole flow.

Three buttons sit at the bottom:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Back | Returns to the country step |
| Skip for now | Finishes onboarding with no bank |
| Finish | Finishes onboarding. The label counts your connections, as in **Finish (1)** |

**Skip for now** and **Finish** do the same thing. Freenary keeps no "finish later" state, so you can connect a bank from Settings at any time.

</Step>

<Step>

### Land on Home

Freenary saves your country, shows the message **You’re all set!**, and opens the Home screen.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">

Home shows one line: **Welcome back, _your name_**. Before your name arrives, the line shows a placeholder and announces **Loading greeting** to a screen reader. Home holds no figures, no charts and no accounts. Every number lives on the Budget screen.

</Callout>

## The First steps checklist

The sidebar footer carries a panel titled **First steps** with a counter, as in `1/3`. Each row links into Settings and marks itself **Done** or **To do**.

| Row | Freenary ticks it when | Where the link goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connect your bank | You have one bank connection or more | Settings, Bank accounts |
| Set up your budget | Your budgeting profile holds one line or more | Settings, Budgeting profile |
| Protect your account | You turn on two-factor authentication, **or** you add one passkey | Settings, Security |

The panel renders nothing until all three answers arrive. Once all three rows read **Done**, the panel holds for a moment and then disappears. It comes back if a row turns to **To do** again, as when you disconnect your last bank or empty your budgeting profile.

## What to do next

Work the checklist in order. A bank connection fills the Budget screen with real transactions. A budgeting profile turns on the **Budget vs. actual** chart. A second factor protects the account.
