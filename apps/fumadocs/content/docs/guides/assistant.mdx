Home is the assistant. Ask it about your accounts, your spending or your budget. It answers from the data your bank already sent to Freenary. This page walks the screen from top to bottom.

**Before you start:** connect a bank. The assistant reads the data Freenary imported, and nothing else. See [Bank connections](/docs/guides/bank-connections).

## What Home shows first

An empty conversation shows four things:

1. The mark, the ring in three colours.
2. The heading **Welcome back, _your name_**.
3. One line of instruction: Ask anything about your accounts, spending or budget.
4. Three suggestion chips.

The three chips read:

- How much did I spend on eating out this year?
- Where did my money go last month?
- Which subscriptions am I paying for?

Press a chip to send that question. You do not have to type it.

## Ask a question

The composer sits under the conversation.

| Element | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| The text box | Holds your question. Empty, it reads **Ask about your money…** |
| **Send** | Sends the question |
| **Stop** | Takes the place of **Send** while the answer arrives. It ends the answer |
| **New conversation** | Sets the conversation aside and opens an empty one |

Freenary prints one line under the composer: Answers come from your own data. Information and arithmetic, not financial advice. Read the assistant that way. It counts, it compares, and it quotes your own figures. It never tells you what to buy or what to sell.

Freenary refuses a question longer than 8000 characters.

## While the assistant works

Send a question, and a row with the mark appears under it at once. It reads **Thinking…** until the first word of the answer arrives. From then on, a status line under the newest content names what the assistant does at that moment:

| Status line | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Thinking…** | The assistant decides what to read, or what to write |
| **Preparing a lookup…** | The assistant writes the arguments of a lookup |
| **Reading your cash flow…** | That lookup runs. With more than one at once, the line adds **with 1 other lookup** |
| **Writing the answer…** | The text of the answer arrives |
| **Drawing a chart…** | The chart arrives |
| **Asking again…** | You pressed **Retry**, and the assistant starts over |

The status line is gone the moment the answer is complete.

## How the assistant answered

The assistant cannot read your money in one step. It thinks, asks your data a question, reads the result, and asks again if it needs more. Each of these questions is a **lookup**. Every answer keeps the steps it took, as a timeline above the text.

The timeline opens while the assistant works and folds when the answer lands, so the answer is what you read. Its header stays: **How the assistant answered**, and on a wide screen the count of steps and lookups and the time the whole answer took. Press the header to open the timeline again. An answer stopped before its first word, and an answer with a failed lookup, keep the timeline open.

Each step is one row, and every row starts at the same edge:

| Row | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| A lookup | A step that made one lookup is that lookup: its name in your language, what it reads on a wide screen, the time it took and its state. Press the row to open it |
| **Reading your data** | A step that made several lookups at once. Each lookup follows as its own row |
| **Answer written** or **Answer written, with a chart** | The last step. **Thinking** is the step in progress, in bold; the step that comes next is in italics |
| The line under a row | **Thought 3s** says the assistant thought before it acted, and for how long. **Uses the results of step 1** says the step reads what the step before it returned. **2 lookups at once** says the lookups ran side by side. Freenary shows what the assistant did, never its private reasoning |
| The time on the right | How long the step or the lookup took. When the line under a row names **Thought**, the thought's time follows it there. Freenary measures every time while the answer arrives, so an older answer shows none after a reload |

A timeline with two lookups or more shows **Expand all** and **Collapse all** above the steps. Press them to open or close every lookup row at once.

### A lookup row

A row names the lookup in your language, says what it reads on a wide screen, and carries a state:

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Preparing** | The assistant writes the arguments of the lookup |
| **Running** | The lookup runs now |
| **Done** | The lookup returned |
| **Failed** | The lookup did not return. The row opens on its own and shows the error text. On the newest answer, once it is complete, the row also offers **Retry** |
| **Cancelled** | You pressed **Stop** while the lookup ran |

Open a row to read two sections under it:

- **Parameters**: the arguments of the lookup, as words and dates. **Raw parameters** opens the exact request, and the button beside it copies it.
- **Result**: one line that says what came back, for example **13 periods** or **2 recurring payments**. **Raw result** opens the exact data, and the button beside it copies it.

Seven lookups exist, and each one asks your data the same question a screen asks:

| Lookup | What it reads |
| --- | --- |
| **Checking your accounts** | Which banks are connected, and the period your transactions cover |
| **Reading your spending by category** | Outgoing totals per category group, over a period |
| **Reading your cash flow** | Money in and money out over time |
| **Splitting fixed and variable spending** | Which part of your outgoings repeats, and which part does not |
| **Comparing your plan with what you spent** | Your budgeting profile against the real figures |
| **Looking for recurring payments** | Repeating payments over the last year, with their rhythm, their usual amount and the next date |
| **Searching your transactions** | Single transactions in a period, by text, category or direction |

A lookup that Freenary does not recognise reads **Reading your data**.

<Callout type="info">

The timeline makes an answer traceable. You see every question the assistant put to your own data, in order, with its arguments and its result. Those figures come from the same code that draws the charts on [Budget](/docs/guides/budget), so a number in the answer and the same number on a chart agree.

</Callout>

## A chart in an answer

When the figures of an answer form a series, the assistant draws one chart under the prose. Several months, several category groups, a plan against the real amount: these get a chart. A question with one figure for an answer gets none.

A chart sits in a panel headed **Chart**. Its button on the right, **View larger**, opens the same chart in a wide side sheet; press **Escape** or the close button to return. Under the header, a titled block holds up to two things:

| Element | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Headline figures | One or more amounts with a label, for example **Total spent**. They sit in a row above the chart |
| The chart | One of three kinds: bars to compare amounts, a line for a trend over time, or a ring for the shares of one total |

Hover a bar, a point or a slice, and a tooltip shows the label and the exact amount. A ring chart lists every slice with its amount beside the ring, and its tooltip adds the share as a percentage.

The chart uses the same colours and the same amount format as [Budget](/docs/guides/budget). The title and the labels are in your language, because the assistant writes them.

A chart holds the figures the assistant quoted when it answered. It does not refresh. Ask again to get a chart for the current data.

<Callout type="info">

The chart appears while the answer arrives, and its header reads **Drawing…** until it is complete. It fills in from the top: the title first, then the figures, then the chart. If the assistant wrote a chart that Freenary cannot draw, the answer shows the line **The chart could not be drawn.** in its place. The prose above it stays readable.

</Callout>

## The mark

The mark stands beside each answer, and it wears the state of the work. It is the same ring that greets you on the sign-in screen. See [Language and appearance](/docs/guides/interface#the-mark).

| What the mark does | When                                                |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| It looks curious   | The text box has focus, or holds your text          |
| It thinks          | Freenary sent your question, or a lookup runs       |
| It speaks          | The text of the answer arrives                      |
| It beams           | The answer landed. The mark holds this for a moment |
| It shows an error  | The answer failed                                   |
| It rests           | Nothing happens                                     |

Only the newest answer's mark moves, and the row that waits for the first word carries it too. The mark on an older answer holds one still frame, and plays no animation.

A question that needs several lookups comes back to the thinking state between them, so a long answer never looks finished while the assistant still reads.

## One conversation

You hold one conversation at a time. Freenary stores it on the server, not in your browser, so it survives a reload and a move to another screen. A short skeleton appears first, and a screen reader hears **Loading your conversation**.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **New conversation** | Sets the current conversation aside and opens an empty one. Freenary deletes nothing |
| **Stop** | Ends the answer and throws it away. A lookup that ran at that moment reads **Cancelled** in the timeline until you leave the screen. The server keeps no part of the answer, so a reload shows no trace |
| **Retry** | Asks the question again and replaces the last answer. Freenary adds no second copy |
| **Copy** | Copies the text of one answer to your clipboard. A chart is not copied |

**Retry** appears on the newest answer only, and only when no answer arrives at that moment. **Copy** appears on every answer that holds text. A failed answer is not stored either, so a reload clears it.

Scroll up in a long conversation, and a button appears: **Scroll to the latest message**. Press it to come back to the foot.

## The language of an answer

The assistant answers in your interface language. It names your categories and your category groups in that language too. Amounts arrive in the same format as the rest of the interface, in euros, unless a lookup reports another currency. See [Language and appearance](/docs/guides/interface).

## What an answer never guesses

An answer holds only the figures the lookups returned.

- Ask about a period your data does not cover, and the assistant tells you so. It gives no estimate for the months your bank did not send.
- Ask before you connect a bank, and the assistant tells you that too. It points you at the bank settings.

## What the assistant cannot do

<Callout type="info">

The assistant reads your data. It changes nothing.

- It cannot correct the category of a transaction. You do that on [Budget](/docs/guides/budget#correct-a-category).
- It cannot start a sync.
- It cannot add a transaction, an account or a budget line.
- It cannot move money.
- It draws one chart per answer at most, and only from the figures it quotes.
- It says nothing until you ask.

</Callout>

## When an answer fails

An error takes the place of the answer, with a **Retry** button beside it.

| Message | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| Too many questions in a row. Wait a moment and try again. | You asked too many questions in a short period |
| This instance has no model configured. An administrator sets AI_BASE_URL and AI_MODEL to switch the assistant on. | Your operator switched the model off while you held the screen open. See [When your instance has no model](#when-your-instance-has-no-model) |
| The assistant could not answer. Try again. | Anything else |

Freenary accepts a limited number of questions from one account in a short period. Wait a moment, then press **Retry**. [The assistant](/docs/self-hosting/assistant) holds the exact number, and the reason for it.

## When your instance has no model

Your operator switches the assistant on. Until then, Home shows one empty state with a plug icon, the title **The assistant is not configured**, and this message on the screen, word for word:

> This instance has no model configured. An administrator sets AI_BASE_URL and AI_MODEL to switch the assistant on.

The screen holds no text box, so you cannot ask anything. Send your operator to [The assistant](/docs/self-hosting/assistant).

## What leaves your instance

Freenary sends your question and the result of every lookup to the model your operator chose, and it never sends an IBAN. [The assistant](/docs/self-hosting/assistant) lists exactly what goes out and what stays.
