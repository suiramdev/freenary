Every transaction sits in one category, and every category sits in one group. On top of that vocabulary you declare a monthly amount per category. This page covers both, and the limits on each.

## The fixed vocabulary

Freenary ships **16 category groups** and **75 categories** inside them. You cannot add, rename or remove either one. The section itself says so: `Categories are grouped, and the groups are fixed. Your own categories can be renamed, recolored, nested under a group, reordered or removed.`

The groups follow the flow of money, from income to outgoings. Each one holds a catch-all category for a movement Freenary places in the group but in no exact category.

| #   | Group           | Catch-all category   |
| --- | --------------- | -------------------- |
| 1   | `Income`        | `Other income`       |
| 2   | `Investments`   | `Other investment`   |
| 3   | `Housing`       | `Other housing`      |
| 4   | `Utilities`     | `Other utilities`    |
| 5   | `Daily living`  | `Other daily living` |
| 6   | `Transport`     | `Other transport`    |
| 7   | `Travel`        | `Other travel`       |
| 8   | `Leisure`       | `Other leisure`      |
| 9   | `Shopping`      | `Other shopping`     |
| 10  | `Subscriptions` | `Other subscription` |
| 11  | `Health`        | `Other health`       |
| 12  | `Education`     | `Other education`    |
| 13  | `Financial`     | `Other financial`    |
| 14  | `Taxes`         | `Other taxes`        |
| 15  | `Transfers`     | `Other transfer`     |
| 16  | `Other`         | `Uncategorised`      |

A built-in category takes the colour and the icon of its group. It carries none of its own.

### Where to read the list

Open Settings, group `Budgeting`, section `Categories`. Every group starts closed, with a badge that counts what is inside it. Open one to read its categories.

A built-in category carries the badge `Built-in` and offers no action. The [Budget](/docs/guides/budget) category filter and the category picker of the transaction drawer list the same 75.

## Custom categories

A custom category is one you add. Unlike a built-in one, it holds a colour and an icon you pick yourself.

Press `New category` in the header of the section. The drawer holds four fields.

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `Name` | 1 to 40 characters. Unique among your own categories, and letter case plays no part in that check |
| `Color` | One of 7 swatches: `Blue`, `Green`, `Grey`, `Orange`, `Pink`, `Purple` and `Red` |
| `Icon` | One of 18 icons, in a grid |
| `Nested under` | One of the 16 groups, or `No parent` |

The 18 icons are `Airplane`, `Arrows`, `Bank`, `Car`, `Coins`, `Dots`, `Film slate`, `First aid kit` and `Fork and knife`. The rest are `Graduation cap`, `House`, `Lightning bolt`, `Safe`, `Receipt`, `Repeat`, `Shield with check`, `Shopping bag` and `Storefront`.

A new category starts blue, with the `Dots` icon, and with no parent. Press `Create category` to save it, or `Cancel` to drop it. You read `Category created`.

<Callout type="warn">

A custom category serves budget lines alone. The transaction picker offers the built-in 75 and none of your own. You cannot file a transaction under a custom category. See [Budget](/docs/guides/budget#fix-a-category).

</Callout>

### Shape a custom category

Each custom row offers four actions. A screen reader reads them as `Edit {label}`, `Delete {label}`, `Move {label} up` and `Move {label} down`. A row in use also carries the badge `{count} line(s)`.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Edit | Re-opens the drawer. `Save changes` writes it, and you read `Category updated` |
| Re-parent | The `Nested under` field moves the category to another group, or out of every group |
| Reorder | Up and down move it inside its group |
| Delete | Removes it, and moves its budget lines |

A custom category with `No parent` stands on its own row in the list, not as a group you can open.

### Delete a custom category

The dialog reads `Delete {label}?`. Under it sits one of two lines:

- `{count} budget line(s) will move to {fallback}.` — the catch-all of its parent group, or `Uncategorised` when the category has no parent.
- `No budget line uses {label}.`

Press `Delete category` to confirm, or `Cancel` to leave. You read `Category deleted`, or `Category deleted — {count} budget line(s) moved to {fallback}`.

A delete touches no transaction, because no transaction ever carried a custom category. Only your budget lines move.

## The budgeting profile

Your budgeting profile is the monthly plan you declare by hand. It is a list of budget lines and nothing else, so there is no profile to create first.

Open Settings, group `Budgeting`, section `Budgeting profile`. Its own line reads `Declare where your money comes from and where it goes each month. A line's category places it in the flow; its name is optional.`

Press `Add a line` for an empty row. A row holds three parts.

| Part | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Optional, and 60 characters at most. Empty, the row shows the label of its category |
| Amount | The planned amount for one month, in euros. A comma or a dot both work |
| Category | One built-in category, or one of your own. A new row holds none |

Your profile holds **200** lines at most.

| Row error | When it appears |
| --- | --- |
| `Enter a positive amount` | The amount is blank, or it is not a number. A zero is a valid amount |
| `Amount is too large` | The amount is past the ceiling of the field |
| `Name must be at most {max} characters` | The name is longer than 60 characters |
| `Pick a category` | The row holds no category, or it names a category you deleted |

<Callout type="info">

The category of a line decides which side of the flow it falls on, and you never pick that side. A category in `Income` counts as a revenue. A category in `Investments` counts as an investment. Every other group, and every custom category with `No parent`, counts as an outgoing.

</Callout>

### Work the editor

- Press the category picker to search with `Search categories…`. An empty result reads `No categories match your search`.
- The picker also offers the row `New category…`. It opens the drawer, and the category you create lands on that line.
- Drag a line by its handle to reorder it. The handle answers the up and down arrow keys too.
- Press the button at the end of a row to remove it.

Above the editor, Freenary draws your plan as a live flow diagram. It runs from your revenues through the category groups to each planned line. It follows what you type. With nothing declared it reads `No budget yet`, over `Add a line below and the flow appears here as you type.`

A summary sits with it: `Revenues:`, then `Allocated:`, then a third figure. That third label flips with its sign. It reads `Money left:` at zero and above, and `Over-allocated:` below it.

### Save the plan

A bar appears at the foot of the page as soon as you change something. It reads `{count} unsaved change(s)`, with `Cancel` and `Save`.

- `Save` is off while any row holds an error. Fix the row first.
- A reorder of the whole list counts as **one** change.
- `Save` writes the whole list at once, and the order on screen becomes the stored order.
- You read `Budgeting profile saved`, or `Failed to save budgeting profile`.

<Callout type="warn">

Your browser warns you before you leave the page with unsaved changes. Save the plan, or press `Cancel`, before you switch language or close the tab.

</Callout>

## What the plan turns on

One budget line or more ticks `Set up your budget` in the sidebar checklist. It also turns on the `Budget vs. actual` chart on Budget. That chart measures each category group against the amount you planned.
