Freenary sorts every transaction into a category, and every category sits in a group. On top of that vocabulary you declare a monthly plan. This page covers both.

## The category groups

Freenary ships **16 category groups** and **75 categories** inside them. Both sets are permanent. You cannot add a group, rename one, or remove one.

The groups follow the flow of money, from income to outgoings:

| #   | Group         | Catch-all category |
| --- | ------------- | ------------------ |
| 1   | Income        | Other income       |
| 2   | Investments   | Other investment   |
| 3   | Housing       | Other housing      |
| 4   | Utilities     | Other utilities    |
| 5   | Daily living  | Other daily living |
| 6   | Transport     | Other transport    |
| 7   | Travel        | Other travel       |
| 8   | Leisure       | Other leisure      |
| 9   | Shopping      | Other shopping     |
| 10  | Subscriptions | Other subscription |
| 11  | Health        | Other health       |
| 12  | Education     | Other education    |
| 13  | Financial     | Other financial    |
| 14  | Taxes         | Other taxes        |
| 15  | Transfers     | Other transfer     |
| 16  | Other         | Uncategorised      |

Each group holds several categories. The catch-all category of a group takes a movement that Freenary places in the group but not in one exact category.

A category has no colour and no icon of its own. It takes the colour and the icon of its group.

<Callout type="info">

The interface holds the full list. Open Settings, group **Budgeting**, section **Categories**. Each group opens as a collapsible row with a count. A category that ships with Freenary carries a **Built-in** badge and offers no actions.

The category picker of the transaction drawer and the category filter of the Budget screen list the same set. See [Budget](/docs/guides/budget).

</Callout>

## Custom categories

A custom category is one you create yourself. Freenary offers it beside the built-in ones.

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Label | 1 to 40 characters. Unique per user, and Freenary ignores letter case in that check |
| Colour | One of 7 colours |
| Icon | One of 18 icons |
| Parent group | One of the 16 groups, or none |
| Order | Move up and move down inside the group |

A new category starts blue, with a neutral icon, and with no parent group. A custom category with no parent group stands as a group of its own in the list.

To create one, open Settings, section **Categories**, and press the create button. You can also create one from a budget line: pick **New category…** on the category picker, and Freenary writes the new category straight onto that line.

<Callout type="warn">

A custom category serves budget lines only. You cannot file a transaction under a custom category. The transaction picker offers the built-in categories alone.

</Callout>

### Delete a custom category

Freenary never leaves a budget line without a category. When you delete a custom category:

1. Freenary moves every budget line of that category to the catch-all of its parent group.
2. The confirmation dialog names that catch-all before you confirm.
3. The result message names the catch-all and counts the lines it moved.

A custom category with no parent group has no group catch-all. Its lines move to **Uncategorised**.

## The budgeting profile

Your budgeting profile is the monthly plan you declare by hand. It is a list of budget lines, and nothing else. There is no separate profile to create.

A budget line holds three things:

| Part | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Optional. With no name, Freenary shows the label of the category |
| Amount | The planned amount per month. Always positive. Type a comma or a dot |
| Category | One built-in category, or one custom category. A new line starts with none |

### How a line becomes revenue, investment or outgoing

You never choose the side of the flow. The group of the category decides it, so the two can never disagree.

| Category group                         | Side of the flow |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Income                                 | Revenue          |
| Investments                            | Investment       |
| Every other group                      | Outgoing         |
| A custom category with no parent group | Outgoing         |

### Edit the profile

Open Settings, group **Budgeting**, section **Budgeting profile**.

- Add a line, and pick its category.
- Drag a line by its handle to reorder it. The handle also answers the up and down arrow keys.
- Delete a line with the button at its end.

Above the editor, Freenary draws your plan as a live flow diagram, with a summary of revenue, allocated amount and the amount left.

A bar appears at the foot of the page as soon as you change something. It counts the changes, and offers **Cancel** and **Save**. A reorder of the whole list counts as one change. **Save** replaces the whole list at once, and the order on screen becomes the stored order.

<Callout type="info">

Freenary warns you before you leave the page with unsaved changes. A language change reloads the page, so Freenary warns you then too. See [Language and appearance](/docs/guides/interface).

</Callout>

## What the plan turns on

One budget line or more ticks **Set up your budget** in the sidebar checklist. It also turns on the **Budget vs. actual** chart of the Budget screen, which measures each category group against its planned amount.
