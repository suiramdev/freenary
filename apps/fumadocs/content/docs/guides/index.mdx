Freenary imports your bank data and shows you where your money goes. This page names every area of the sidebar, tells you which ones work today, and points you to the right guide.

## What you can do today

- Create an account, and protect it with a second factor or a passkey.
- Connect a bank, and import bank accounts, balances and transactions.
- Ask the assistant on Home about your accounts, your spending and your budget.
- Read the Budget screen, and correct the category of a transaction.
- Declare a monthly plan, and add your own categories.
- Choose a language and an appearance.

## The sidebar

The sidebar lists six areas. Three of them work. Three of them open a placeholder screen.

| Area | Address | State | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | `/` | Built. It holds the assistant. | [The assistant](/docs/guides/assistant) |
| Portfolio | `/portfolio` | Planned | — |
| Budget | `/budget` | Built | [Budget](/docs/guides/budget) |
| Analysis | `/analysis` | Planned | — |
| Goals | `/goals` | Planned | — |
| Settings | `/settings` | Built | [Settings](/docs/guides/settings) |

<Callout type="info">

Freenary marks Portfolio, Analysis and Goals with a **Planned** badge in the sidebar, and dims their labels. Each one opens a single placeholder screen with a title and one line of text. These three areas hold no data. You cannot enter data into them, and no other screen reads from them.

</Callout>

## What a page cannot do

Two limits apply to every screen.

| Limit | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| Freenary imports data from a bank only. | You cannot add an account, a transaction or an asset by hand. |
| Freenary has no scheduler. | A sync runs when you open the Budget screen, or when you press a sync button. |

## Where the operator decides

An operator runs the Freenary instance you sign in to. Four of their choices change what you see:

- The bank provider. With no bank provider, the bank list stays empty. See [Bank connections](/docs/guides/bank-connections).
- The email delivery settings. With no email delivery, Freenary sends no one-time code. See [Signing in](/docs/guides/signing-in).
- The sign-in providers. Freenary hides a button for a provider the operator left unset.
- The model behind the assistant. You pick the model on Home. With no model on the server, the list offers the models that run on your own device alone. See [The assistant](/docs/guides/assistant).
