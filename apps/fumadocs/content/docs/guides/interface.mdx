Two controls change how Freenary looks and reads. Neither one lives in Settings. This page covers both, and the mark that reacts to you.

## Language

Freenary ships in **English** and **French**. The menu names each language in its own words: **English** and **Français**.

### Where to change it

| Screen                      | Control                                     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| The sign-in screen          | A language button above the form            |
| The onboarding wizard       | A language button in the header             |
| Any screen once you sign in | The sidebar user menu, submenu **Language** |

### What happens when you change it

1. Freenary stores your choice in a cookie in your browser.
2. The page reloads.
3. The whole interface comes back in the new language.

<Callout type="warn">

The reload is real. If you have unsaved changes in the Budgeting profile editor, Freenary warns you first. Save or cancel before you switch. See [Categories and budget lines](/docs/guides/categories#edit-the-profile).

</Callout>

### What follows the language

| Text | Follows your choice? |
| --- | --- |
| Every label, button and message | Yes |
| Country names in the onboarding list, and their order | Yes. Your browser supplies them |
| Category and category-group labels | Yes |
| Amounts and dates | Yes. The format follows the language |
| The label of your own custom category | No. Freenary shows the text you typed |
| Bank names and BIC codes | No. They come from your bank |
| The name of an operator sign-on provider | No. The operator sets it |

<Callout type="info">

Freenary keeps no language column on your account. The choice lives in the cookie of that browser. A different browser starts from the language your browser asks for, and falls back to English.

</Callout>

## Appearance

Freenary offers three appearances, in this order:

| Choice | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| System | Follows the light or dark setting of your operating system, and keeps following it |
| Dark | Dark, always |
| Light | Light, always |

**System** is the default.

### Where to change it

The same three screens carry it:

- The sign-in screen, on a button above the form.
- The onboarding wizard, in the header.
- The sidebar user menu, submenu **Theme**.

The button names the control, not your current choice, because the browser reads the stored choice after the first paint.

Unlike the language, an appearance change needs **no reload**. Freenary swaps the appearance in place.

## The mark

The mark is a ring in three colours: green, orange and blue. Freenary draws it from numbers, not from an image file. It is the icon of your browser tab, the mark at the top of the sidebar, and the face above the sign-in form.

The ring opens into a face. Point at the mark at the top of the sidebar, or reach it with the Tab key. The ring then becomes a pair of eyes and a smile. Move away and it closes back to the plain ring. It is no control beyond the link home.

On the sign-in screen the face answers what you do.

| What you do            | What the face does                  |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Nothing                | It rests.                           |
| Enter a text field     | It looks curious.                   |
| Type                   | It listens.                         |
| Enter a password field | It closes its eyes.                 |
| Send the form          | It turns into a loading ring.       |
| Meet a refusal         | It shows an error for a moment.     |
| Sign in                | It beams while the next page loads. |

The face is decoration. The form's own messages, spinners and highlights tell you the same thing.

<Callout type="info">

The mark respects the reduced-motion setting of your operating system. With reduced motion on, Freenary holds one still frame of each expression and plays no animation.

</Callout>

## Your avatar

The circle beside your name in the sidebar shows up to two initials from your name. Freenary has no avatar upload, and no image URL. See [Settings](/docs/guides/settings#what-settings-does-not-hold).
