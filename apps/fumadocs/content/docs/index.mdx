Freenary is an open-source personal finance app that you host yourself. It connects your banks and imports your accounts and transactions. It files each transaction under a category, and shows a Budget area beside your own monthly plan. Every figure lives in the PostgreSQL database of your own instance. One exception: each question you ask the assistant sends financial data to the model endpoint the operator configured. Read [Assistant](/docs/self-hosting/assistant).
