Freenary's mark is a ring split into three colors — green, orange and blue. It is the icon in your browser tab, the first thing on the sign-in screen, and the first thing in the sidebar, above the navigation. It is also a character: the ring opens into a face, and the same shape becomes every expression and every busy state you will see elsewhere in the app.

## Where you see it

| Place | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Browser tab** | The plain ring, as the tab and bookmark icon. |
| **Sign-in screen** | The face, above the form, watching you fill it in. The other half of the screen is the plain ring, large, on a field of smaller ones. |
| **Top of the sidebar** | The same ring, next to the Freenary name, linking to Home. |
| **Beside every answer on Home** | The [assistant](/docs/ai) wearing the state of what it is doing. |
| **Collapsed sidebar** | The ring alone, filling the rail; the name moves into a tooltip. |

## It answers you

On the sign-in screen the face follows what you are doing. It turns towards a field you are in, listens while you type, and closes its eyes while you enter a password. Submitting spins it into a loading ring; a refused sign-in makes it flinch and go red for a moment, and a successful one makes it beam on the way to your home page.

In the sidebar, point at the mark — or reach it with the keyboard — and the ring opens into a pair of eyes and a smile. Move away and it closes back to the plain ring.

Every one of these is decoration, not information: the form's own messages, spinners and highlights say the same thing, and nothing behaves differently whether the animation plays or not.

<Callout type="info">
  If your system asks for reduced motion, the mark holds a single still frame
  for each expression instead of animating between them. Nothing moves across
  the screen.
</Callout>

## It is the assistant's face

On [Home](/docs/home) the same mark is what the assistant answers with, and its state is the state of the work rather than decoration:

| What it shows | What is happening |
| --- | --- |
| Looks up at you | You are writing a question. |
| Shrinks, looks aside, a dot circling it | It is looking something up in your data. |
| Its mouth opens and closes | It is writing the answer. |
| Green sweeps the whole ring and it smiles | The answer just landed. |
| Shakes, turns red, knits its brows | Something failed — the answer says what. |

A question that needs several lookups goes back to the thinking face between them, so a long answer does not look finished while it is still reading.

## More expressions exist

The mark can also look excited, surprised, confused, sleepy or shy, celebrate, wink, or shrink to a dot that marks something new. Those are waiting on the screens that will have a reason to show them.

## Light and dark

The face takes its color from the [appearance](/docs/appearance) you chose, so it stays legible on either background; the three ring colors are the brand's and do not change. The tab icon is the plain ring, with no face to color, so it looks the same either way.
