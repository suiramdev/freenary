Freenary's mark is a piggy bank: a round teal body, two eyes and a coin slot across the crown. It is the icon in your browser tab and the first thing in the sidebar, above the navigation.

## Where you see it

| Place | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Browser tab** | The resting mark, as the tab and bookmark icon. |
| **Top of the sidebar** | The same mark, next to the Freenary name, linking to Home. |
| **Collapsed sidebar** | The mark alone, filling the rail; the name moves into a tooltip. |

## It answers you

Point at the mark — or reach it with the keyboard — and it looks up, catches a coin through its slot, and settles into a pleased squint. Move away and it returns to resting. It also blinks now and then while you leave it alone.

The greeting is decoration, not information: the link behaves exactly the same whether the animation plays or not, and its own highlight is what tells you it is focused.

<Callout type="info">
  If your system asks for reduced motion, the mark changes expression without
  the coin, the tilt or the blinking. Nothing moves across the screen.
</Callout>

## Light and dark

The mark takes its colors from the [appearance](/docs/appearance) you chose, so it stays legible on either background. The tab icon does the same, following your browser's own light or dark chrome rather than the setting inside Freenary — a tab icon is drawn before the page is.
