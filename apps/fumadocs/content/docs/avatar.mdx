Freenary's mark is a ring split into three colors — green, orange and blue. It is the icon in your browser tab and the first thing in the sidebar, above the navigation. It is also a character: the ring opens into a face, and the same shape becomes every expression and every busy state you will see elsewhere in the app.

## Where you see it

| Place | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Browser tab** | The plain ring, as the tab and bookmark icon. |
| **Top of the sidebar** | The same ring, next to the Freenary name, linking to Home. |
| **Collapsed sidebar** | The ring alone, filling the rail; the name moves into a tooltip. |

## It answers you

Point at the mark — or reach it with the keyboard — and the ring opens into a pair of eyes and a smile. Move away and it closes back to the plain ring.

The greeting is decoration, not information: the link behaves exactly the same whether the animation plays or not, and its own highlight is what tells you it is focused.

<Callout type="info">
  If your system asks for reduced motion, the mark holds a single still frame
  instead of animating. Nothing moves across the screen.
</Callout>

## More expressions exist

The mark can also look happy, curious, surprised, confused, sleepy or concerned, think, listen, speak, celebrate, spin as a loading indicator, or shrink to a dot that marks something new. None of those appear on their own yet — nothing in Freenary decides when to show them, so today the sidebar greeting is the only one you can trigger. They are here for the [assistant](/docs/ai), which will need a face for what it is doing.

## Light and dark

The face takes its color from the [appearance](/docs/appearance) you chose, so it stays legible on either background; the three ring colors are the brand's and do not change. The tab icon is the plain ring, with no face to color, so it looks the same either way.
