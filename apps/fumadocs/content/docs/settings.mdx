Settings is where you configure your financial profile by hand, without connecting anything. It works on a fresh account with no bank account linked.

## Budgeting profile

A budgeting profile is what you _plan_ each month, declared in three ordered groups: **revenues**, then **investments**, then **outgoings**. Each line carries a name, a monthly amount, and a category.

A Sankey diagram above the form updates as you type, reading left to right:

**revenues → Budget → category → line**

The middle column groups your lines by the category you assigned, so several outgoings sharing one category appear as a single band that splits into its lines. Investment categories are drawn before outgoing ones, matching the order you enter them.

Whatever your revenues do not cover appears as a grey **Unallocated** band, and the strip under the diagram totals revenues, allocated and unallocated. Allocate more than you earn and that strip flips to **Over-allocated** — the plan is still saved; nothing is clamped.

Amounts are entered in major units (`500` means €500) and saved as whole cents. Saving replaces the whole profile at once, so reordering or removing a line takes effect on save, not before.

## Categories

The same section lists the seventeen built-in categories — Dining, Education, Entertainment, Groceries, Health, Housing, Income, Insurance, Other, Savings, Shopping, Subscriptions, Taxes, Transfers, Transport, Travel and Utilities — each marked **Built-in** and not editable.

Alongside them are your own categories. Create one from the Categories section, or straight from a budget line's category picker when none of the existing ones fit. A custom category has a name unique to your account, a color drawn from the chart palette, an icon, and optionally a built-in category to nest under; nested categories are indented beneath their parent and can be reordered within it.

Custom categories can be renamed, recolored, re-iconed, re-parented, reordered and deleted. Each row shows how many budget lines currently use it.

Deleting one never orphans a budget line. Before the category goes away, every line using it is moved to its parent category, or to **Other** when it has no parent — the confirmation tells you exactly how many lines will move and where.

<Callout type="info">
  Custom categories apply to your budgeting profile. Categorizing an imported
  transaction still uses the built-in categories only.
</Callout>
