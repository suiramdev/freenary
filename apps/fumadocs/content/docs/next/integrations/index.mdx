An integration connects Freenary to an outside service that carries data. The bank integration is the one that matters. It brings your accounts, your balances and your transactions in, and it keeps them fresh.

<Callout type="warn">

**Powens is the recommended integration.** It lets Freenary pull and sync bank transactions, accounts and balances automatically.

No screen in Freenary accepts a transaction by hand. So an instance with no bank integration has no data to show, and every screen stays empty.

</Callout>

## What a bank integration carries

| Freenary imports | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Bank accounts | The accounts you share at the bank, with the name and the number the bank reports |
| Balances | Recorded each time the provider reports one |
| Transactions | Every transaction inside the sync window, filed under a category |
| Investment holdings | Recorded on an investment account, and only through Powens |

Freenary talks to no bank itself. A bank provider holds that connection, and the operator of the instance picks the provider. [Configuration](/docs/self-hosting/configuration) holds the variable that names it.
