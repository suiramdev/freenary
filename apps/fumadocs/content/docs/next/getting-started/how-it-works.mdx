---
title: How Freenary Works
description: The path from one bank to one budget figure, and every word the Freenary interface uses along it.
---

Freenary moves your money data along one path. An operator runs an instance. You hold an account on it. Your account links a bank. That link imports accounts and transactions. Freenary files each transaction under a category, and the Budget screen reports the result.

Each word below keeps the same meaning on every page of this website, and in the interface. Every line names the page that owns the behaviour behind the word.

## The instance you sign in to

Freenary runs nowhere until somebody installs it. That copy is the instance, and it decides which options you get.

- **Instance** — one installed copy of Freenary, with its own users and its own database. Read [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting).
- **Operator** — the person who runs an instance and picks its options. Read [Configuration](/docs/self-hosting/configuration).

## The account you hold

Your account is your way into one instance. It carries who you are, and every method that proves it.

- **Account** — your own way into an instance. It holds your name, your email address and every way you sign in. Read [Signing in](/docs/guides/signing-in).
- **One-time code** — a 6-digit code that Freenary emails you. It confirms an address or resets a password, and it lives 10 minutes. Other tools call it an OTP. Read [Signing in](/docs/guides/signing-in).
- **Passkey** — a key your device keeps. Your device unlock signs you in, and you type no password. Read [Signing in](/docs/guides/signing-in).
- **Two-factor authentication** — a code from your authenticator app, on top of your password. Recovery codes stand in when you lose the app. Read [Settings](/docs/guides/settings).
- **Single sign-on** — sign-in through the identity provider that your operator connected. The button reads `Continue with single sign-on`, or the name the operator gave it. Read [Signing in](/docs/guides/signing-in).

## The data your bank sends

Every figure in Freenary starts at a bank. A connection brings the accounts, and each sync brings the transactions those accounts booked.

- **Bank connection** — one link between Freenary and one of your banks. It holds the accounts that bank shared. Read [Connect a bank](/docs/integrations/powens#connect-a-bank-account).
- **Bank account** — one account inside a bank connection, such as a current account, a savings account or a card. Read [Connect a bank](/docs/integrations/powens#connect-a-bank-account).
- **Sync** — the read that brings new transactions and balances from your bank into Freenary. Read [Connect a bank](/docs/integrations/powens#connect-a-bank-account).
- **Transaction** — one entry your bank booked on an account. It carries a date, an amount and the text the bank sent. Read [Budget](/docs/guides/budget).
- **Company** — the other side of a transaction, as Freenary reads it from that text. The Budget filter named `Company` lists the companies of the period. Read [Budget](/docs/guides/budget).
- **Internal transfer** — a pair of transactions that moves money between two of your own accounts. Freenary matches the pair and files both under `Internal transfer`. Read [Budget](/docs/guides/budget).

## The budget Freenary builds from it

A raw transaction list answers little. Freenary labels each transaction, adds your plan on top, and reports both over the period you choose.

- **Category** — the label one transaction carries, such as `Groceries`. Read [Categories](/docs/guides/transactions#categories-and-budget-lines).
- **Category group** — the wider family a category sits in, such as `Housing`. The 16 groups are fixed. Read [Categories](/docs/guides/transactions#categories-and-budget-lines).
- **Custom category** — a category you create, with your own name, colour and icon, under a group of your choice. Read [Categories](/docs/guides/transactions#categories-and-budget-lines).
- **Budget line** — one monthly amount you plan, on one category. Read [Categories](/docs/guides/transactions#categories-and-budget-lines).
- **Budgeting profile** — all your budget lines together. Its side of the flow comes from the group of each line. Read [Categories](/docs/guides/transactions#categories-and-budget-lines).
- **Period** — the date range the Budget screen shows: one month, three months or one year. Read [Budget](/docs/guides/budget).
- **Aggregation** — how a period of many months reports a figure: `Total`, `Monthly avg.` or `Monthly median`. Read [Budget](/docs/guides/budget).
- **Fixed and variable spending** — the two rows of the `Fixed vs. variable` chart. `Fixed` is the spending that repeats; `Variable` is the rest. Read [Budget](/docs/guides/budget).
- **Uncategorised** — the category a transaction keeps while no rule, no dictionary entry and no model decide one for it. Read [Categories](/docs/guides/transactions#categories-and-budget-lines).

## The assistant that reads it back

The assistant answers in words what the Budget screen answers in charts. It reads the same data.

- **Assistant** — the part of Home that answers a question about your money from your own data. Each question also reaches the model endpoint the operator configured. Read [The assistant](/docs/guides/assistant).
- **Conversation** — one thread of questions and answers with the assistant. The `New conversation` action files the current thread away. Read [The assistant](/docs/guides/assistant).

## Money

A transaction carries the currency its bank booked it in. The figures of the period and the two totals of the transaction list are in euros.
