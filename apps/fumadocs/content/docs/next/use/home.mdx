Home is the screen you land on after you sign in. Its cards answer one question each about your money, from the balances and transactions your banks send. It fills once you have a [bank connection](/docs/use/bank-connections); with none, it offers `Connect a bank`.

Press `Sync transactions`, the round button above the cards, to fetch new data from your banks.

## Net Worth

**Net Worth** is what you own minus what you owe: every account balance added up, with a balance below zero, such as a loan or an overdraft, counted as a debt. When you have debts, the card shows your assets and your debts under the total.

The bar under the total splits your assets into kinds of account, with each one's amount and share:

| Kind | Accounts |
| --- | --- |
| `Cash` | Current accounts and cards |
| `Savings` | Savings accounts |
| `Investments` | Brokerage, life insurance, retirement, employee savings and crowdlending |
| `Property` | Real estate |
| `Other` | Any other account |

`Balances as of` gives the latest time a bank reported a balance.

## Available to Spend

**Available to Spend** is what your current accounts hold, minus the commitments still due before the end of the month. Commitments are the payments on a regular cadence for a steady amount that **Recurring** lists under `Commitments`. Spending patterns, such as groceries, aren't taken off: they are what this amount is for.

The figure turns red, with a minus sign, when your current accounts don't cover the commitments due this month.

## Spent This Month

**Spent This Month** adds up every payment out of your accounts since the first of the month. It's the same figure as the expenses on **Transactions** for this month, internal transfers included.

The line under it compares your spending so far with the same day last month. The chart draws both months day by day: this month as a solid line, last month as a dashed one, with last month's total in the legend.

## Upcoming Payments

**Upcoming Payments** lists the commitments due in the next 14 days, with how many there are and their total. It shows the first five, soonest first; the rest are on **Recurring**.

## Top Categories This Month

**Top Categories This Month** shows the four categories you spent the most in this month, with each one's share of your spending. Open **Transactions** to see every category.

## Investments

**Investments** adds up your investment accounts: brokerage, life insurance, retirement, employee savings and crowdlending. It lists your three largest holdings. When your bank reports what you paid for them, the card also shows your unrealised gain or loss, in money and as a share of what you paid.

## Accounts

**Accounts** lists every account with its balance in the account's own currency, its bank and its kind. A dash means the bank hasn't reported a balance. `Bank connections` opens the place where you add or remove a bank.

## Currencies

**Net Worth** and **Investments** count only the accounts in the currency most of your accounts use. **Net Worth** tells you how many accounts it leaves out, either because they're in another currency or because their bank reported no balance.

**Available to Spend** uses the currency most of your current accounts use. So does the line above the list on **Upcoming Payments**: its count and total cover only the commitments in that currency, and it's hidden when none is. The list itself shows every commitment, each in its own currency. **Spent This Month** and **Top Categories This Month** work like **Transactions**: an amount in another currency is added as it is. **Accounts** shows each balance in its own currency.

Freenary doesn't convert between currencies.
