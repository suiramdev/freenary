Home is the screen you land on after you sign in. It starts with what you have now: your balance, your accounts and your latest transactions. The cards under them cover this month, and **In detail**, at the bottom, holds your net worth, your spending pace and your investments. It fills once you have a [bank connection](/docs/use/bank-connections); with none, it offers `Connect a bank`.

On a phone, a bar at the bottom of the screen opens **Home**, **Transactions**, **Recurring** and **Settings**. On a wider screen, the sidebar does.

## Current accounts

The large amount at the top is what your current accounts hold, added up. It turns red, with a minus sign, when they are overdrawn. `Updated` gives the latest time a bank reported the balance of one of these current accounts. Press `Sync transactions`, the round button next to it, to fetch new data from your banks.

## Accounts

**Accounts** shows one tile per account with its name, its bank and its balance in the account's own currency. A dash means the bank hasn't reported a balance. On a narrow screen, scroll the row sideways to see every tile.

Press a tile to open the account: its full name, its bank and kind, its balance and its transactions, newest first. `Show older transactions` loads more. The last tile, `Bank connections`, opens the place where you add or remove a bank.

## Recent transactions

**Recent transactions** lists your six latest transactions across every account, with the company, the day and the category. Money in shows in green with a plus sign; money out shows with a minus sign.

Freenary names a transaction after the other party your bank reports, or after the company it reads from the bank's label. Press a transaction to see its date, the bank's full label and its category, and to [fix the category](/docs/use/budget#fix-a-category). `All transactions` opens **Transactions**.

## Available to spend

**Available to spend** is what your current accounts hold, minus the bills still due before the end of the month. Bills are the payments on a regular cadence for a steady amount that **Recurring** lists under `Bills`. Spending patterns, such as groceries, aren't taken off: they are what this amount is for.

The figure turns red, with a minus sign, when your current accounts don't cover the bills due this month.

## Spent this month

**Spent this month** adds up every payment out of your accounts since the first of the month. It's the same figure as the expenses on **Transactions** for this month, internal transfers included. The line under it compares your spending so far with the same day last month.

## Upcoming bills

**Upcoming bills** lists the bills due in the next 14 days, with how many there are and their total. It shows the first five, soonest first; the rest are on **Recurring**.

## Top categories this month

**Top categories this month** shows the four categories you spent the most in this month, with each one's share of your spending. Spending Freenary hasn't sorted into a category is left out of the list. A line under it gives that amount, and `Review unsorted transactions` opens **Transactions** filtered to it.

## In detail

### Net worth

**Net worth** is what you own minus what you owe: every account balance added up, with a balance below zero, such as a loan or an overdraft, counted as a debt. When you have debts, the card shows your assets and your debts under the total.

The bar under the total splits your assets into kinds of account, with each one's amount and share:

| Kind | Accounts |
| --- | --- |
| `Cash` | Current accounts and cards |
| `Savings` | Savings accounts |
| `Investments` | Brokerage, life insurance, retirement, employee savings and crowdlending |
| `Property` | Real estate |
| `Other` | Any other account |

`Balances as of` gives the latest time a bank reported a balance.

### Spending pace

**Spending pace** draws this month's spending and last month's, day by day: this month as a solid line, last month as a dashed one, with last month's total in the legend. It starts on the second day of the month.

### Investments

**Investments** adds up your investment accounts: brokerage, life insurance, retirement, employee savings and crowdlending. It lists your three largest holdings. When your bank reports what you paid for them, the card also shows your unrealised gain or loss, in money and as a share of what you paid.

## Currencies

The amounts under **Current accounts** and **Available to spend** use the currency most of your current accounts use. So does the line above the list on **Upcoming bills**: its count and total cover only the bills in that currency, and it's hidden when none is. The list itself shows every bill, each in its own currency. **Spent this month** and **Top categories this month** work like **Transactions**: an amount in another currency is added as it is. **Accounts** and **Recent transactions** show each amount in its own currency.

**Net worth** and **Investments** count only the accounts in the currency most of your accounts use. **Net worth** tells you how many accounts it leaves out, either because they're in another currency or because their bank reported no balance.

Freenary doesn't convert between currencies.
