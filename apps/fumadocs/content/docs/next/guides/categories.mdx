Every transaction sits in one category, and every category sits in one group. On top of that vocabulary you declare a monthly amount per category. This page covers both, and the limits on each.

## The fixed vocabulary

Freenary ships **3 category groups** and **27 categories** inside them. You cannot add, rename or remove either one. The section itself says so: `Categories are grouped, and the groups are fixed. A category takes the color of its parent. Your own categories can be renamed, reordered or removed, and nested under a group or under one of your top-level categories. Nesting stops at one level.`

A group is a section of the flow of money, and not a topic. `Income` is what arrives. `Savings & investment` and `Spending` are the two ways it leaves.

| Group | What it holds | Categories | Catch-all category |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Income` | Money that arrives | 7 | `Other income` |
| `Savings & investment` | Money you put aside | 4 | `Savings` |
| `Spending` | Money you consume | 16 | `Other` |

A category carries an icon of its own, and it takes the colour of its group. Two categories of one group therefore share a colour, and the icon tells them apart.

### The 7 income categories

| Category | It covers |
| --- | --- |
| `Salary & wages` | Pay from an employer |
| `Self-employment & business income` | What your own activity brings in |
| `Investment income` | Interest, dividends and gains you are paid |
| `Rental income` | Rent a tenant pays you |
| `Benefits & pension` | A benefit, an allowance or a pension |
| `Refunds & reimbursements` | Money sent back to you |
| `Other income` | Money in that fits none of the six above |

### The 4 savings and investment categories

| Category | It covers |
| --- | --- |
| `Savings` | Money moved to a savings account |
| `Investments` | Shares, funds and securities you buy |
| `Retirement & long-term savings` | A pension plan, and a life-insurance savings contract |
| `Crypto` | A purchase on a crypto platform |

### The 16 spending categories

| Category | It covers |
| --- | --- |
| `Groceries` | Food shops and household supplies |
| `Restaurants & going out` | Restaurants, bars, cafés and takeaway |
| `Rent & mortgage` | Your rent, or the loan on your home |
| `Bills & utilities` | Energy, water, telecom, home upkeep and home insurance |
| `Car & fuel` | Fuel, upkeep, parking, tolls and car insurance |
| `Transport & travel` | Public transport, taxis, flights and hotels |
| `Shopping` | Clothes, electronics, furniture, gifts and pets |
| `Entertainment & leisure` | Culture, sport and hobbies |
| `Subscriptions` | A charge that repeats under a contract |
| `Health & medical` | Doctors, pharmacy and personal care |
| `Family & education` | Childcare, school and courses |
| `Taxes` | A tax or a duty you owe |
| `Loans & bank fees` | Loan repayments and what your bank charges |
| `People` | Money you send to, or take from, a person you know |
| `Cash withdrawal` | Cash out of a machine or over a counter |
| `Other` | Anything Freenary places nowhere else |

Insurance holds no category of its own, because one word covers three different needs. Car insurance falls under `Car & fuel`. Home, health and liability insurance fall under `Bills & utilities`. A life-insurance savings contract falls under `Retirement & long-term savings`.

<Callout type="info">

A movement between two of your own accounts is not a category. Freenary matches the pair, marks both as an internal transfer, and keeps the category each one already carried. Read [Budget](/docs/guides/budget).

</Callout>

### Which categories a transaction offers

A category belongs to one side of the flow, and the picker reads the sign of the transaction.

- On money in, it offers the 7 income categories, over `People`, `Cash withdrawal` and `Other`. That is 10 rows.
- On money out, it offers the 4 savings categories and the 16 spending ones. That is 20 rows.

So `Salary & wages` never appears on a card payment, and `Groceries` never appears on a credit. The three rows in both lists are the ones that run both ways. You lend a friend money and you get money back, and cash leaves a machine or returns to the counter.

### Where to read the list

Open Settings, pane `Budget`, section `Categories`. Every group starts closed. Open one to read its categories.

A built-in category is a plain row. It offers no action, and the [Budget](/docs/guides/budget) category filter lists the same 27.

## Custom categories

A custom category is one you add. It holds an icon that you pick, and a colour that follows the same rule as a built-in one.

Press `New category` in the header of the section. The dialog holds four fields.

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `Name` | 1 to 40 characters. Unique among your own categories, and letter case plays no part in that check |
| `Nested under` | One of the 3 groups, one of your own top-level categories, or `No parent`. Each row carries the icon of that parent, and the field shows the icon of the parent you chose |
| `Color` | One of 7 swatches: `Blue`, `Green`, `Grey`, `Orange`, `Pink`, `Purple` and `Red`. The field shows only while `Nested under` is `No parent` |
| `Icon` | One of 28 icons, in a grid |

A category that you nest takes the colour of its parent. The 27 built-in ones do the same. The colour tells you if money comes in, is put aside, or goes out. A nested category therefore cannot show a different colour. A category with `No parent` is a group of its own. A group holds its own colour, so you pick it.

The first ten icons are `Airplane`, `Arrows`, `Bank`, `Bitcoin`, `Briefcase`, `Car`, `Cash`, `Pie chart`, `Coins` and `Dots`. The next ten are `Film slate`, `First aid kit`, `Fork and knife`, `Graduation cap`, `House`, `Key`, `Lightning bolt`, `Safe`, `Plant` and `Receipt`. The last eight are `Refund`, `Repeat`, `Shield with check`, `Shopping bag`, `Shopping cart`, `Storefront`, `Trend line` and `People`.

A new category starts blue, with the `Dots` icon, and with no parent. Press `Create category` to save it, or `Cancel` to drop it. You read `Category created`.

<Callout type="warn">

A custom category serves budget lines alone. The transaction picker offers the built-in 27 and none of your own. You cannot file a transaction under a custom category. See [Budget](/docs/guides/budget#fix-a-category).

</Callout>

### Shape a custom category

Each custom row offers four actions. A screen reader reads them as `Edit {label}`, `Delete {label}`, `Move {label} up` and `Move {label} down`. A row in use also carries the badge `{count} line(s)`.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Edit | Re-opens the dialog. `Save changes` writes it, and you read `Category updated` |
| Re-parent | The `Nested under` field moves the category to another group, to one of your top-level categories, or out of every parent. The colour follows the new parent, and the `Color` field comes back when you move the category out of every parent |
| Reorder | Up and down move it among the categories that share its parent |
| Delete | Removes it, and moves its budget lines |

A custom category with `No parent` stands on its own row. It opens like a group as soon as it holds a subcategory.

### Subcategories

You can nest one of your own categories under another one of your own. The `Nested under` list holds `No parent`, then the 3 fixed groups, then your own top-level categories.

Two rules hold the tree to one level.

- Only a top-level category accepts subcategories. A category that already sits under a parent is in no `Nested under` list.
- A category that holds subcategories takes no parent of its own. Move each subcategory out first, and the category is free again.

A subcategory sits under its parent in the list, and a guide line joins it to that parent. It shows the colour of the parent, which is the colour the parent itself carries. Freenary keeps the colour you picked. It comes back with the `Color` field when you move the category out of every parent.

A subcategory serves budget lines the same way every other custom category does.

### Delete a custom category

The dialog reads `Delete {label}?`. Under it sits one of two lines:

- `{count} budget line(s) will move to {fallback}.` — the catch-all of its parent group, or `Other` for a top-level category and for a subcategory.
- `No budget line uses {label}.`

A category that holds subcategories adds a third line:

- `Its {count} subcategories are deleted with it.`

That budget-line count covers the parent and its subcategories. Each of those lines moves.

Press `Delete category` to confirm, or `Cancel` to leave. You read `Category deleted`, or `Category deleted — {count} budget line(s) moved to {fallback}`.

A delete touches no transaction, because no transaction ever carried a custom category. Only your budget lines move.

## The budgeting profile

Your budgeting profile is the monthly plan you declare by hand. It is a list of budget lines and nothing else, so there is no profile to create first.

Open Settings, pane `Budget`, section `Budgeting profile`. Its own line reads `Declare where your money comes from and where it goes each month. A line's category places it in the flow; its name is optional.`

Press `Add a line` for an empty row. A row holds three parts.

| Part | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Optional, and 60 characters at most. Empty, the row shows the label of its category |
| Amount | The planned amount for one month, in euros. A comma or a dot both work |
| Category | One built-in category, or one of your own. A new row holds none |

Your profile holds **200** lines at most.

| Row error | When it appears |
| --- | --- |
| `Enter a positive amount` | The amount is blank, or it is not a number. A zero is a valid amount |
| `Amount is too large` | The amount is past the ceiling of the field |
| `Name must be at most {max} characters` | The name is longer than 60 characters |
| `Pick a category` | The row holds no category, or it names a category you deleted |

<Callout type="info">

The category of a line decides which side of the flow it falls on, and you never pick that side. A category in `Income` counts as a revenue. A category in `Savings & investment` counts as an investment. Every category in `Spending` counts as an outgoing, and so does every custom category that sits under no group.

</Callout>

### Work the editor

- The category picker reads `Pick a category` while the row holds none. Type in it to narrow the list, and an empty result reads `No categories match your search`.
- Type a name that no category carries, and the picker adds the row `New category…`. It opens the dialog, and the category you create lands on that line.
- Drag a line by its handle to reorder it. The handle answers the up and down arrow keys too.
- Press the button at the end of a row to remove it.

Above the editor, Freenary draws your plan as a live flow diagram. It runs from your revenues through the three groups to each planned line. It follows what you type. With nothing declared it reads `No budget yet`, over `Add a line below and the flow appears here as you type.`

A summary sits with it: `Revenues:`, then `Allocated:`, then a third figure. That third label flips with its sign. It reads `Money left:` at zero and above, and `Over-allocated:` below it.

### Save the plan

A bar appears at the foot of the page as soon as you change something. It reads `{count} unsaved change(s)`, with `Cancel` and `Save`.

- `Save` is off while any row holds an error. Fix the row first.
- A reorder of the whole list counts as **one** change.
- `Save` writes the whole list at once, and the order on screen becomes the stored order.
- You read `Budgeting profile saved`, or `Failed to save budgeting profile`.

<Callout type="warn">

Your browser warns you before you leave the page with unsaved changes. Save the plan, or press `Cancel`, before you switch language or close the tab.

</Callout>

## What the plan turns on

One budget line or more ticks `Set up your budget` in the sidebar checklist. It also turns on the `Budget vs. actual` chart on Budget. That chart measures each category against the amount you planned for it.
