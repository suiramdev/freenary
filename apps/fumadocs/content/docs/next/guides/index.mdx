---
title: Using Freenary
description: The guides that cover every screen of a running Freenary instance.
---

These guides cover the screens of a running instance. Each one names the controls on the screen, the text they carry, and what happens when you press them.

The sidebar holds one group, `Application`, with six rows: `Home`, `Portfolio`, `Budget`, `Analysis`, `Goals` and `Settings`. `Budget` opens two pages of its own. Three rows — `Portfolio`, `Analysis` and `Goals` — carry the badge `Planned`. Each of the three is a working link to a page that holds a title and one line of text.
