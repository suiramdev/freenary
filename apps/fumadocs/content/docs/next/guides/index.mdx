---
title: Using Freenary
description: The eight guides that cover every screen of a running Freenary instance.
icon: Compass
---

A running instance links your bank, imports your accounts and your transactions, and files each transaction under a category. You read the result on the Budget screen. You declare a monthly plan, and you ask the assistant about your money. Freenary imports every figure from your bank, so you type no account and no transaction by hand.

The sidebar holds one group, `Application`, with six rows. Three of them — `Portfolio`, `Analysis` and `Goals` — carry the badge `Planned`. Each of the three is a working link. Each one opens a page that holds a title and one line of text, with no control on it.
