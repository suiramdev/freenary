Home is the assistant. Ask it about your accounts, your spending or your budget. It answers from the data your bank already sent to Freenary, and from nothing else.

**Before you start:** connect a bank, or the assistant has little to read. See [Bank connections](/docs/guides/bank-connections).

## The empty conversation

A fresh conversation holds four things:

- The large Freenary mark, with the accessible name `Freenary's assistant`.
- The heading `Welcome back, {name}`, with your own name.
- The line `Ask anything about your accounts, spending or budget.`
- Three suggestion chips.

The chips read `How much did I spend on eating out this year?`, `Where did my money go last month?` and `Which subscriptions am I paying for?` Press one and Freenary sends that question. The chips stay off until a model is ready to answer.

## The composer

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Text box | Holds your question. Empty, it reads `Ask about your money…` |
| Submit | A screen reader reads it as `Send`, or as `Stop` while the answer arrives |
| Model button | Names the model that answers your next question |
| `New conversation` | Sets the current thread aside and opens an empty one |
| Scroll button | Appears in a long thread. A screen reader reads it as `Scroll to the latest message` |

A question holds 8000 characters at most.

## Two states, and which one you are in

The model button tells you which state your instance is in.

<Tab value="A model on the instance">

Your operator set a model up. The button names that model, and the text box works at once.

Press the button to open a palette titled `Model`, with the search box `Search models…`. The group `On your instance` holds the model your operator set. A browser that reports WebGPU also shows the group `On this device`. A search that matches nothing reads `No model matches.`

<Tab value="No model on the instance">

Your operator set none. The button reads `Choose a model`. The text box, the submit button and the chips are all off. They come on when you pick a model for your own browser.

One line sits above the composer. With WebGPU it reads `Choose a model to start. It downloads once from Hugging Face, runs on your graphics card, and your questions stay in this browser.`

Without WebGPU it reads:

```text
This browser has no WebGPU, which the assistant needs to run a model on this
device. Use a current Chrome, Edge or Safari, or ask an administrator to set
AI_BASE_URL and AI_MODEL.
```

The palette then holds no `On your instance` group. Without WebGPU it holds no `On this device` group either, so nothing but the search box remains.

## A model in your own browser

The group `On this device` lists the models your browser can download and run. Each one shows the graphics memory it needs, in gigabytes. Pick one and the download starts at once.

- The button reads `Loading {model}` with a percentage while it downloads.
- Your browser keeps the model, so a later visit downloads nothing.
- Freenary remembers your choice in that browser. A remembered model on your device wins over the one on the instance.
- A download that fails reads `The model could not be loaded: {reason}`, with `Retry` beside it.
- While the catalogue loads, the palette reads `Finding the models this device can run…`. A failure reads `The list of models could not be loaded.`

## What leaves your instance

Each question sends your question and the results of its lookups to the model that answers. Those results hold your own financial data: your accounts, your transactions, your spending by category and your plan.

- A model on the instance receives that data at the endpoint your operator set. See [The assistant](/docs/self-hosting/assistant).
- A model in your own browser keeps the whole conversation in that browser.

A question sent to an instance with no model reads:

```text
This instance has no model configured. An administrator sets AI_BASE_URL and
AI_MODEL to switch the assistant on.
```

That is a message on your screen, and the names in it are for your operator. You change nothing from here.

## Read an answer

Each answer keeps its steps above the text. The header reads `How the assistant answered`, or `How the assistant is answering` while the answer arrives. `Expand all` and `Collapse all` open and fold every step.

Each question the assistant puts to your own data is a lookup. Seven exist, and each one names itself:

| Lookup | What it reads |
| --- | --- |
| `Checking your accounts` | Your accounts |
| `Searching your transactions` | Your transactions |
| `Reading your spending by category` | Spending per category |
| `Reading your cash flow` | Money in against money out |
| `Splitting fixed and variable spending` | The fixed share of your spending |
| `Looking for recurring payments` | Payments that repeat |
| `Comparing your plan with what you spent` | Your plan against the real amounts |

A lookup Freenary cannot name reads `Reading your data`.

While the answer arrives, a status line names the work of that moment. It reads `Thinking…`, `Asking again…` on a retry, `Preparing a lookup…`, `Writing the answer…` or `Drawing a chart…`.

### A chart in an answer

An answer can carry a chart. It sits in a panel headed `Chart`, with the action `View larger`. The panel shimmers with `Drawing…` while the answer still writes it. A chart the assistant cannot draw reads `The chart could not be drawn.`

### Actions on an answer

- `Copy` copies the text of a settled answer to your clipboard.
- `Retry` asks the question again. It appears on the newest answer alone.

### The mark beside an answer

The Freenary mark sits beside the live answer and wears one state. One rule picks it, and the first match wins:

1. An error on the answer.
2. A thought, while the question waits or while a lookup runs.
3. Speech, while the text arrives.
4. Success, on an answer that landed.
5. Curiosity, while the composer is live.
6. Rest, otherwise.

## What the assistant cannot do

The assistant reads. It writes nothing back.

- It changes no data: no category, no budget line, no account and no transaction.
- It starts no sync. Press `Sync transactions` on [Budget](/docs/guides/budget#sync-from-this-screen) yourself.
- It moves no money.

You hold one conversation at a time. There is no list of past ones. Press `New conversation` and Freenary sets the current thread aside. It opens an empty one, with no way back to the thread you set aside.

## When an answer fails

The error takes the place of the answer, with `Retry` beside it.

| What you read | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| `Too many questions in a row. Wait a moment and try again.` | Wait, then press `Retry` |
| `This conversation is too long for the model on this device. Start a new conversation.` | Press `New conversation` |
| `The model on this device could not answer. Try again, or pick a different model.` | Press `Retry`, or pick a smaller model |
| `The assistant could not answer. Try again.` | Press `Retry` |
