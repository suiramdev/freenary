Two controls change how Freenary reads and how it looks. Neither one sits in Settings. This page names where each control lives, and what changes when you use it.

## Where the two controls live

Three screens carry them, and no other screen does.

| Screen | Where the controls sit |
| --- | --- |
| The sign-in screen | Two buttons above the form: the appearance first, then the language |
| The setup | Two buttons in the header, before `Sign out` |
| Every screen once you sign in | The sidebar account menu, under your name |

The account menu holds one submenu each: `Language`, then `Theme`, then the `Sign out` item.

<Callout type="warn">

Settings holds neither control. It has three groups — `Security`, `Connections` and `Budgeting` — and none of them touches the language or the appearance. See [Settings](/docs/guides/settings).

</Callout>

## Language

Freenary reads in two languages, and no third one ships with it. The menu names each one in its own words:

| Choice  | The menu reads |
| ------- | -------------- |
| English | `English`      |
| French  | `Français`     |

On the sign-in screen and in the setup header, the language button reads the name of your current language. It does not read the word `Language`. Inside the account menu the submenu is labelled `Language`, and a radio mark sits beside your current choice.

### Change it

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the control

Press your name at the foot of the sidebar, then open the `Language` submenu. Before you sign in, press the language button instead.

</Step>

<Step>

### Pick the other language

The page reloads, and the whole interface comes back in the language you picked. The reload is what makes the server agree with your choice.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">

The reload is real, and it drops unsaved work. Save your budgeting profile, or press `Cancel` on the unsaved-changes bar, before you switch. See [Categories and budget lines](/docs/guides/transactions#save-the-plan).

</Callout>

Your choice belongs to that browser. Another browser starts in the language it asks for, and falls back to English.

### What follows the language

| Text | Follows your choice |
| --- | --- |
| Every label, button and message | Yes |
| The country names in the setup list, and their order | Yes |
| The category and category-group labels | Yes |
| Dates | Yes. The format follows the language |
| Amounts | Yes. The format follows the language, and the currency stays the euro |
| An answer from the assistant | Yes. Freenary asks it to answer in your language |
| The name you gave your own category | No. Freenary shows the text you typed |
| Bank names | No. They come from the service that links your bank |
| The name of a single sign-on provider | No. Your operator sets it |

## Appearance

Three choices sit in the `Theme` submenu, in this order:

| Choice | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `System` | Follows the light or dark setting of your operating system, and changes with it |
| `Dark` | Dark, always |
| `Light` | Light, always |

`System` is the default.

The trigger names the control rather than your choice: it reads `Theme` on all three screens. The browser must read your stored choice first, so the trigger cannot name it.

An appearance change needs **no reload**. Freenary swaps the appearance in place, so unsaved work survives it.
