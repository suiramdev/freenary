`Portfolio` is the second row of the `Application` group in the sidebar. It carries the badge `Planned`, and its row is dimmer than the rows beside it. A screen reader reads the row as `Portfolio (planned)`.

The row is a working link. Press it and Freenary opens a page with three things on it, centred and nothing else:

- A wallet icon.
- The heading `Portfolio is planned`.
- One line under it: `Track your holdings, accounts and net worth across all your connections — coming soon.`

The page holds no control, no figure and no table. Nothing on it reads your data.

## What Freenary keeps about an investment account

An investment account arrives with a bank connection, like every other account. Freenary records the securities inside it when the linking service returns them. Each one carries a label, a code, a quantity, a unit value and a valuation.

Each sync replaces that record with what the service reports at that moment. A position you sold goes away with it, so Freenary holds one snapshot and no history.

<Callout type="info">

Only one of the two linking services returns your securities. Ask your operator which one your instance uses. See [Bank providers](/docs/integrations/powens#setup).

</Callout>

## Where your investment money renders today

The `Investments` category group carries the movements your investment accounts booked. A transfer to a brokerage account and a dividend both land on Budget. A budget line in that group counts as an investment, and not as an outgoing.

See [Budget](/docs/guides/budget) and [Categories and budget lines](/docs/guides/transactions#categories-and-budget-lines).
