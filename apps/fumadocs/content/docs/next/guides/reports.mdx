---
title: Reports and insights
description: What the Analysis row of the sidebar opens, and the screens that report on your money today.
---

`Analysis` is the fourth row of the `Application` group in the sidebar. It carries the badge `Planned`, and its row is dimmer than the rows beside it. A screen reader reads the row as `Analysis (planned)`.

The row is a working link. Press it and Freenary opens a page with three things on it, centred and nothing else:

- A bar-chart icon.
- The heading `Analysis is planned`.
- One line under it: `Evaluate whether your financial setup is healthy and efficient — coming soon.`

The page holds no control, no figure and no chart. Nothing on it reads your data.

## The screens that report on your money

| Screen | What it reports |
| --- | --- |
| [Budget](/docs/guides/budget) | Income, expenses and the difference over the period you pick, with two charts beside them |
| [Recurring transactions](/docs/guides/transactions#recurring-transactions) | A strip of measured insights, six figures, and what your commitments cost a month and a year |
| [The assistant](/docs/guides/assistant) | The same figures in words, from a question you type |

The `Budget vs. actual` chart of Budget measures each category group against the plan you declared. It is the one screen that reads your plan against your real transactions.
