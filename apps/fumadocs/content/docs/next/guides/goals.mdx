title: Goals
description: What the Goals row of the sidebar opens, and where to plan a monthly amount today.
---

`Goals` is the fifth row of the `Application` group in the sidebar. It carries the badge `Planned`, and its row is dimmer than the rows beside it. A screen reader reads the row as `Goals (planned)`.

The row is a working link. Press it and Freenary opens a page with three things on it, centred and nothing else:

- A target icon.
- The heading `Goals is planned`.
- One line under it: `Define financial objectives, track progress and plan contributions — coming soon.`

The page holds no control, no figure and no form. Nothing on it reads your data.

## Where to declare an amount

The screen that takes a monthly amount from you is the budgeting profile in Settings. One line there names a category and the amount you plan for it each month. The `Budget vs. actual` chart then measures each category group against that plan.

See [Categories and budget lines](/docs/guides/transactions#categories-and-budget-lines) and [Budget](/docs/guides/budget).
