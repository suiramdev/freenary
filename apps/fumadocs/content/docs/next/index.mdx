---
title: Freenary
description: Freenary is an open-source personal finance app that you host yourself, with your data in your own database.
---

Freenary is an open-source personal finance app that you host yourself. It connects your banks, imports your accounts and transactions, and files each transaction under a category. Every figure lives in the PostgreSQL database of your own instance.

This site holds one copy of the documentation for each version of Freenary. The dropdown at the top of the sidebar switches between them. The account menu of your own instance opens the version it runs.
