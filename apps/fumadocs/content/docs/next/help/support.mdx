title: Get support
description: Where to open an issue, which of the three forms to pick, and the facts each one asks for.
---

Freenary has one place for a question, a bug and a request: the issue tracker of the repository. Read [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting) and [Frequently asked questions](/docs/help/faq) first. Most symptoms already carry a fix there.

## Open an issue

Start at [github.com/suiramdev/freenary/issues/new/choose](https://github.com/suiramdev/freenary/issues/new/choose). Blank issues are off, so pick one of three forms.

| Form | Use it for | Title prefix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Bug report` | Something behaves in a way the documentation does not describe | `[Bug]:` |
| `Feature request` | A workflow you want, and the problem behind it | `[Feature]:` |
| `Other` | A question, or anything the first two do not fit | `[Other]:` |

Search the open issues before you write a new one. A comment on the issue that matches carries more weight than a duplicate.

## What the bug form asks

Two fields sit at the top, and the first one is mandatory.

| Field | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| `Area` | `Web app`, `Server / API`, `Database / migrations`, `Docker / local setup` or `Other` |
| `Version or commit` | The branch or commit you run, such as `main @ abc1234` |

Add these to the details, and your report needs no follow-up question:

- What you did, what you expected, and what happened.
- The exact error text, or the log lines around it.
- The output of `docker compose ps -a`.
- How you serve TLS, and which origins you set.
- The variables you set, with every secret removed.

<Callout type="warn">

An issue is public. Remove every secret, every access token and every bank identifier before you paste a log line. [Logs](/docs/self-hosting/logs) names what the output holds.

</Callout>

## What the feature form asks

Three fields, and the first two are mandatory. The form wants the problem before the solution.

| Field | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| `Problem or use case` | What you try to do, and what is missing |
| `Proposed solution` | The change you want to see |
| `Alternatives or additional context` | Workarounds, related tools or mockups |

## Send a fix instead

A pull request is welcome, and the repository states what it expects of one. Read [Contributing](/docs/developers/contributing) for the tools, the checks and the pull request rules.

A documentation page that is wrong counts as a bug. [Writing documentation](/docs/developers/contributing#writing-documentation) holds the authoring rules and the two gates every page passes.
