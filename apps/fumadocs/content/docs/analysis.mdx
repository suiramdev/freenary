Analysis asks: **"How healthy and efficient is my financial situation?"** This is where Freenary moves beyond aggregation and transforms financial data into actionable understanding.

## Fees

Investment costs appear at several levels: fund expense ratios, brokerage fees, management fees, insurance-wrapper fees, transaction costs and advisory fees. Freenary combines them so users understand not just individual percentages but their long-term impact — for example, how much a 1.8% annual fee might reduce an investment's value over ten, twenty or thirty years under configurable assumptions. Abstract percentages become understandable financial consequences.

## Diversification

Diversification analysis evaluates portfolios across geography, sectors, asset classes, currencies and individual underlying companies. Apparently diversified portfolios can hide substantial overlap: several global and technology ETFs may share significant exposure to the same small group of companies.

## Dividends

Dividend analysis tracks received distributions, expected income, dividend yield and income evolution over time — helping income-focused investors understand how much of their portfolio return comes from distributions.

## Risk

Risk analytics may eventually include volatility, drawdowns, concentration risk, liquidity exposure, currency exposure and scenario-based stress testing. The objective is not to overwhelm users with professional metrics, but to let advanced analytics exist underneath clear explanations produced by the interface and AI.
