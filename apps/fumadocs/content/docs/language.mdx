Freenary's interface is available in **English** and **French**. The choice belongs to you, not to your account: it is remembered in your browser and applies before you have signed in.

## Choosing a language

The language control appears in the three places where you might need it:

- On the **sign-in screen**, above the form — so you can read the form before you have an account.
- In the **onboarding header**, next to Sign out.
- In the **sidebar**, under your name, as **Language** in the account menu.

Each one offers the same list, showing every language in its own name — English, Français. Picking one reloads the page in that language.

## What follows the choice

The language you pick also decides how figures and dates are written, so a French interface shows `1 234,56 €` and `3 février 2026` where an English one shows `€1,234.56` and `February 3, 2026`. Country names in the onboarding list are translated too, and sorted alphabetically in the language you are reading.

What stays as it was: anything that comes from your bank or from you. Bank names, account names, transaction labels and the categories you created keep the wording their source gave them.

## The first visit

Before you have chosen anything, Freenary reads the language your browser asks for. A browser set to French gets a French interface on the very first page; anything else gets English.

<Callout type="info">
  The choice is stored in a cookie, so it is per browser rather than per
  account. Signing in on another device starts from that device's browser
  language until you choose again there.
</Callout>

## Adding a language

Only English and French ship today. A new language is a contribution rather than a setting — the [web app's contributor guide](https://github.com/suiramdev/freenary/blob/main/apps/web/AGENTS.md) covers what one takes.
