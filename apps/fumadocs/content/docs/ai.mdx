Because Freenary holds structured information about your accounts, transactions and budget, it can be asked questions in plain language instead of navigated. The assistant lives on [Home](/docs/home): type a question, and it looks the answer up in your own data before writing it.

## Asking a question

Home opens on the mark and a text box. Ask anything the financial model can answer:

- "How much did I spend on eating out this year?"
- "Where did my money go last month?"
- "Which subscriptions am I paying for?"
- "Am I over my plan on groceries?"

The assistant answers in the language you chose in [Language](/docs/language), and amounts arrive formatted the way the rest of the interface formats them.

## What it looks up

The assistant cannot see your data directly. It has a fixed set of lookups, and each one asks Freenary the very same question a screen asks:

| Lookup | What it reads |
| --- | --- |
| **Checking your accounts** | Which banks are connected, and the period your transactions cover. |
| **Reading your spending by category** | Outgoing totals per category group, over a period. |
| **Reading your cash flow** | Money in and money out over time. |
| **Splitting fixed and variable spending** | Which part of your outgoings recurs and which does not. |
| **Comparing your plan with what you spent** | Your [budgeting profile](/docs/settings#budgeting-profile) against reality. |
| **Looking for recurring payments** | Subscriptions and other repeating payments over the trailing year. |
| **Searching your transactions** | Individual operations, filtered by text, category or direction. |

Each lookup appears in the conversation as a row you can open, named in your language, showing whether it succeeded. That is the point: an answer is traceable to the questions that produced it, and the figures come from the same code that draws the charts in [Budget](/docs/budget) — the assistant and the charts cannot disagree.

## The mark answers with you

The [mark](/docs/avatar) sits beside every answer and shows what the assistant is doing: it shrinks and looks aside while it reads your data, its mouth moves while it writes, the ring goes green when the answer lands, and it shakes and turns red when something fails. It also looks up while you are typing.

## One conversation

Home keeps one conversation, and it survives a reload — reopening Freenary picks up where you left off. **New conversation** starts a fresh one; the old thread is set aside rather than deleted.

## Explainability

Freenary distinguishes information, mathematical simulation and regulated financial advice. The assistant states figures it read from your data and never invents one: if the period you asked about is outside the data you have, it says so rather than extrapolating. Every answer carries the reminder that it is information and arithmetic, not advice.

## Self-hosted instances

The assistant needs a model, and Freenary ships with none: an instance whose operator has not configured one shows "The assistant is not configured" on Home instead of a text box. Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint works, including a model running on your own hardware — see [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting#ai-assistant).

Questions are rate-limited per account, because every question costs the operator money at their provider.
