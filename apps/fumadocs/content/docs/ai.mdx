Artificial intelligence is a fundamental component of Freenary. Because Freenary holds structured information about accounts, transactions, investments, goals and historical behavior, an AI system can reason across the user's complete financial context.

## Answering questions in plain language

- "How much did I spend on restaurants during the last six months?" — translated into an analysis of categorized transactions.
- "Can I afford a €3,000 holiday in October?" — considering cash balances, expected income, upcoming recurring expenses and other goals.
- "Why did my net worth decrease this month?" — separating spending, investment losses, debt changes and account movements.

More sophisticated questions span multiple areas: "If I increase my ETF investment to €800 per month, when could I reach €500,000?" or "Would increasing my mortgage payment or investing the difference have been more advantageous historically?" The AI layer can combine financial calculators, projections and actual financial data into structured scenarios.

## Proactive insights

Freenary can surface observations without being asked: savings falling for several consecutive months, significant spending shifts, unusually concentrated portfolios, disproportionately high fees, or cash accumulating far beyond the safety target.

## Explainability

Freenary clearly distinguishes information, mathematical simulation and regulated financial advice. AI responses expose their assumptions, and calculations remain inspectable rather than presenting uncertain projections as guarantees.

Explainability is central: when the AI states spending increased by 22%, the user can open the underlying transactions; when it says a portfolio is concentrated in technology, the holdings responsible are inspectable. The AI functions as **an interface to the underlying financial model**, never as an opaque source of answers.
